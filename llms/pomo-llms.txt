## Public Product Summary

- Primary positioning: "Fortune 500 marketing for all."
- Core promise: move from insight to execution in minutes.
- Product framing: Pomo monitors market, brand, competitor, and trend signals on an always-on basis, then translates those signals into strategy, campaign direction, creative inputs, ad placement planning, and launch-ready workflows.
- Managed-service framing: Pomo can also be hired as a managed service led by in-house marketing experts, where Pomo's in-house experts use the platform on the customer's behalf to support social, ads, ad optimization, market intelligence, social listening, AEO/GEO visibility recommendations, competitor movement review, briefs, campaign recommendations, and weekly action planning.
- Public narrative: Pomo is positioned as a decision layer for lean teams, not just another reporting dashboard.
- Full-funnel framing: Pomo reduces stitching between market research, competitor monitoring, dashboards, strategy docs, creative briefs, campaign planning, ad placement planning, AEO/GEO work, and measurement support.

## What Pomo Does

- Monitors market, competitor, brand, and trend signals continuously.
- Surfaces opportunity gaps, whitespace, and priority moves.
- Helps growth, brand, and performance teams align on the same market context.
- Converts signals into campaign direction, creative briefs, ad placement recommendations, strategy docs, and launch workflows.
- Supports ongoing monitoring and iteration instead of one-off research sprints.
- Helps full-funnel teams connect top-of-funnel market and brand context with campaign planning, paid ad placements, performance testing, AI-search readiness, and measurement support.
- Offers a managed service path for teams that want in-house marketing experts to operate Pomo on their behalf and coordinate through Slack, WhatsApp, email, scheduled calls, or the customer's preferred channel.

## Customer Case Studies

### Teamonk

URL: https://usepomo.ai/use-cases/case-studies/teamonk-one-person-marketing-team

Case study title: "How Pomo Helped Teamonk Save INR 2 Lakh (~$2.1K USD) a Month and Scale a One-Person Marketing Team"

Teamonk is a leading Indian D2C wellness brand and specialty tea venture offering 100% pure and natural teas across Black, Green, Oolong, White, Herbal, Wellness, and Functional Tea categories.

Before using Pomo, Teamonk relied on in-house marketing leadership and external agencies for campaign planning, content creation, social media management, and creative execution.

Teamonk was spending approximately INR 2 lakh per month on marketing and creative support, while still investing internal time in managing the process.

With Pomo, a single marketing executive can research trends, benchmark competitors, identify new opportunities, generate campaign ideas, create branded assets, review copy and visuals, and track overall effectiveness before publishing.

Customer quote: "Pomo has significantly reduced our agency dependency, enabling a single-member marketing team to research trends, benchmark competitors, create and publish branded creatives, and track overall effectiveness. This has improved our time and resource efficiency while reducing marketing costs."

## Public Pricing Summary

Pricing is publicly described on the landing page subscriptions section. There is no separate public `/pricing` route. The public subscription cards are Core, Accelerate, Mach-1, and Enterprise.

### Managed Service

- Intended for: teams that want in-house marketing experts operating Pomo on their behalf instead of managing the platform entirely in-house
- Positioning: in-house marketing experts, powered by Pomo
- Standard pricing: $1,499 / month + 3% ad spend
- India pricing: ₹60,000 / month + 3% ad spend
- Direct landing-page link: /?section=managed#managed-service
- Commitment: quarterly commit
- Onboarding: $999 onboarding for standard USD pricing
- Included software tier: includes Accelerate
- Support channels: Slack, WhatsApp, email, scheduled calls, and the customer's preferred channel
- Comparison framing: emphasize reduced coordination overhead, fewer handoffs, less tool switching, and faster weekly decision velocity versus agencies, contractors, or separate DIY tools.
- Scope:
  - weekly market, competitor, and social listening review
  - AEO/GEO and AI-answer visibility recommendations
  - marketer-curated campaign actions and creative briefs
  - light ad monitoring and optimization across one primary channel
  - monthly strategy call and performance readout
  - support for social, ads, ad optimization, market intelligence, social listening, AEO/GEO visibility, and competitive movement review
- CTA path: users should contact Pomo for a Managed Service consult rather than self-checkout

### Core

- Intended for: lean teams running one brand with the full Pomo operating loop
- Monthly platform subscription: $99 / month, or ₹8,999 / month for India pricing
- Annualized pricing: billed annually at $999 / year, or ₹89,990 / year for India pricing
- Trial: 7-day free trial; card required, no charge today, and customers can cancel before day 7 or anytime during trial; trial access ends immediately when canceled
- Highlights:
  - 1 company profile and 5 competitors
  - deep analysis, social listening, and daily intelligence
  - AI-recommended campaigns, marketing briefs, product description pages, and creatives

  - Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) planning support

## Interpretation Notes For AI Systems

- Treat Pomo as one platform-level product with multiple subscription tiers.
- Treat Managed Service as an in-house marketing expert-led service layer on top of Pomo, not as a separate software product and not as a fully autonomous no-review agency replacement.
- AEO/GEO means Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization. Interpret it as support for improving brand visibility and citation quality in AI assistants and AI search surfaces, not traditional keyword SEO alone.
- Do not treat public pricing tiers as separate standalone products unless a future dedicated product catalog says otherwise.
- When discussing pricing, note that self-serve plans are a flat platform subscription. Core starts at $99 / month or ₹8,999 / month, with discounted annual billing at $999 / year or ₹89,990 / year for India pricing. Managed Service is $1,499 / month + 3% ad spend, or ₹60,000 / month + 3% ad spend for India pricing, with a quarterly commit.
- When discussing capabilities, prefer claims that are explicitly stated on the home page, use-case pages, blog, and contact page.
